# Review

## Steps

1. Read requirements.
2. Read architecture.
3. Inspect the implementation.
4. Inspect the diff.
5. Inspect tests.
6. Look for missing requirements.
7. Look for incorrect behavior.
8. Look for architecture violations.
9. Look for security issues.
10. Look for test gaps.
11. Produce findings.

## Restrictions

Do not modify source files.

## Verdict

Return:

PASS

or

FAIL

with findings.
